                    Security checklist

                    Your balance is growing. These optional checks help keep a self-custody wallet safer — they do not block sending.

                    • Recovery phrase verified Confirmed when you created this wallet, or after restore.
                    • Auto-lock enabled Locks after inactivity so the passphrase is required again.
                    • TLS certificate verify on Protects Electrum connections from man-in-the-middle attacks.
                    • Know the wipe path Resetting local data requires typing a confirmation phrase. Funds are only recoverable with your written recovery phrase.

                    Explorer

                    Display unit
                    Home balance tap cycles LTC → litoshis → hidden.

                    Insights

                    Insights fetches public metrics from your explorer host (IP visible). Wallet addresses are never uploaded.

                    Contacts

                    Names and addresses stay on this computer. Reusing a Public address can link payments to a person — prefer a fresh transparent address when privacy matters.

                      No contacts yet.

                      Metadata backup

                      Contacts and labels are not in your recovery phrase. Export them before wiping or moving to another computer, then import after restore. Merge never deletes local entries that are missing from the file.

                      Diagnostics

                      Share this with support when sync is stuck. It includes connection settings, sync phase, and recent non-secret log lines — never your recovery phrase or passphrase.

                      What leaves this computer

                      • Electrum — learns your transparent addresses and your IP.
                      • MWEB peers — learn your IP (not your transparent address list).
                      • Explorer (litview) — learns your IP when fetching spot price, fee hints, or Insights pulse/charts; IP and a specific txid when you open transaction details or click “View on litview”. Wallet address lists are never uploaded.
                      • LitVM RPC — the Caldera (or custom) HTTP endpoint learns your IP and your LitVM 0x address when you open the LitVM tab or send zkLTC.
                      • On disk — transparent wallet metadata in sqlite is plaintext. Use FileVault (or other full-disk encryption) on this Mac.

                      There is no Tor or proxy support yet. Turning off Insights, “Show LTC/USD”, and fee-rate suggestions limits litview contact to actions you start (detail view or “View on litview”). This wallet does not make you anonymous on the network.

                      Connection

                      Known servers
                      Protects against man-in-the-middle attacks. Some community Electrum servers use self-signed certificates and only work with verification off. Turn this off if you run your own Electrum server for privacy — it stops the wallet from ever sending your addresses to public servers.

                      MWEB data

                      Rebuild the local MWEB coin database from scratch. This re-downloads the full UTXO set and can take a while.
